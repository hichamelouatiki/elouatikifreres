AI for giants: how Elouatiki Frères is revolutionizing dam inspection

June 30, 2023·7 min read

In civil engineering, the durability of critical infrastructure relies on flawless monitoring. Under the colossal weight of millions of cubic metres of water, concrete dams are a permanent maintenance challenge: they endure extreme hydrostatic pressure, freeze-thaw cycles and slow but relentless chemical erosion.

At Elouatiki Frères, we understood that traditional inspection methods are no longer enough to guarantee optimal safety. Our teams recently deployed a cutting-edge solution combining high-tech drones and Deep Learning to radically transform the inspection of these giants. Here is how we are redefining the standards of predictive maintenance.

Why manual inspection has become the weak link

For decades, dam safety rested on the human eye. Expert rope-access technicians, suspended over the void, scrutinised the wall millimetre by millimetre. As respectable as that craft is, it now has three major limitations that we have chosen to overcome:

  1. Subjectivity: an inspection report can vary from one expert to another. The interpretation of how serious a crack is is often human, and therefore variable.
  2. Operational risk: working at height remains the leading cause of severe accidents. Reducing field-team exposure is both an ethical and a legal priority.
  3. Slowness and cost: inspecting a face of several hectares by hand is a process that stretches over weeks, tying up valuable resources.

Our solution: field expertise meets high-end technology

For this project Elouatiki Frères did not simply « take photos ». We deployed a real flying laboratory able to operate in hostile environments. Near a dam, aerological turbulence and the magnetic disturbance from high-voltage transformers make piloting complex.

Our specialised pilots fly DJI Matrice 350 RTK platforms. Equipped with a full-frame 45-megapixel gimbal (Zenmuse P1), this drone captures details invisible to the naked eye from the ground. For zones where the wall geometry is complex or hidden by vegetation, we add LiDAR sensors to map the structure with millimetre accuracy. Where classical inspection sees a uniform grey wall, our technology documents the « skin » of the concrete, building a raw dataset of unprecedented density.

An end-to-end controlled operational workflow

The added value of Elouatiki Frères lies in the rigour of our data-processing chain. We structured our intervention around three technological pillars:

1. High-precision acquisition (GNSS RTK)

Error has no place in dam safety. To guarantee that inspections are repeatable, we use automated flight plans powered by RTK (Real-Time Kinematic). Backed by a ground base station, we correct the drone's position in real time with centimetre accuracy.

  • The benefit? Every shot is geo-referenced precisely in space. We can come back two years later and re-take the photo from the exact same angle to the millimetre, finally turning temporal comparison into a scientific exercise.

2. The Digital Twin: the structure's digital double

Once the thousands of images are captured, our engineers use photogrammetry to generate a Digital Twin. This ultra-faithful 3D model is more than just a picture: it is a spatial database. Asset managers can navigate the structure from their desk, measure distances, compute degradation volumes and view the entire face without the optical distortions of a regular photograph. It is the ultimate tool for stakeholder discussion and planning.

3. The brain (AI): semantic segmentation, pixel-level accuracy

This is where Elouatiki Frères' expertise becomes disruptive. Manually reviewing 5,000 high-resolution photos would take weeks. Our Deep Learning algorithms do it in a few hours with tireless rigour. We combine two complementary approaches:

  • Detection (YOLO / Faster R-CNN): to instantly locate disorder « objects » (rebar corrosion, honeycombing, efflorescence).
  • Semantic segmentation (U-Net): the finest of the two. Instead of just detecting a crack, the AI traces it pixel by pixel. We can then automatically extract vital metrics: length, orientation and above all the opening (the width) of the crack, even when it is only 0.2 mm wide.

The severity scale: steering maintenance budgets with confidence

AI does not replace the engineer; it lets them focus on the critical zones. To make decision-making easier, our reports classify every detected pathology:

  • Class 1

    Microcracks and crazing

    • Characteristic: surface-level openings.
    • Action: archived in the digital twin for monitoring during the next campaign.

  • Class 2

    Stable structural cracks

    • Characteristic: active cracks with no leakage.
    • Action: kinematic analysis through temporal overlay.

  • Class 3

    Critical cracks or infiltration

    • Characteristic: wide cracks with visible moisture.
    • Action: immediate alert, priority human review and planning of sealing or injection works.

Business benefits: an undeniable ROI

By trusting Elouatiki Frères with the inspection of your assets, you turn a regulatory constraint into a strategic advantage:

  • Speed of execution: inspections completed 5 to 10 times faster than classical methods, minimising site downtime.
  • Total safety: fall-from-height risk is virtually removed for both your team and ours.
  • Predictive maintenance: by overlaying data from successive inspections we surface degradation trends before they trigger heavy, costly repairs.
  • Objectivity and auditability: our algorithms do not get tired. They review the 5,000ᵗʰ photo with the same precision as the first, delivering an impartial, auditable report.

Conclusion: the drone, a new pillar of industrial decisions

The drone is no longer an image-capture accessory; at Elouatiki Frères it has become a strategic data sensor plugged into a powerful digital ecosystem. By blending our operational know-how with the power of Deep Learning, we deliver an augmented, objective view of infrastructure health.

More than just a technical service, we deliver real decision-making intelligence. AI does not replace human expertise — it gives that expertise the means to protect and sustain our concrete giants for the decades to come.
